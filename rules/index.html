How to play Yamb

Yamb, called Jamb in Croatia, Serbia, Bosnia and Herzegovina and Slovenia, is a dice game for one to six players. It is close to Yahtzee, with one twist that makes it deeper: the score sheet has several columns, and each column is filled in its own order.

A turn

Take turns. Roll 5 dice up to three times, keeping any dice between rolls, then write the result into one box. If the dice don’t fit the box, it scores 0. When every sheet is full, the highest total wins.

The columns

Yamb Café starts with four columns. Each one holds the same boxes, from the ones down to Yamb.

↓
Down. Fill from the top box to the bottom, in order.
↑
Up. Fill from the bottom box to the top, in order.
↕
Free. Any box, in any order.
N
Call. Right after your first roll, call a box in this column. You must write it this turn, by your third roll at the latest.

Scoring

1 – 6Sum of the dice showing that number
Bonus for 1–6+30 when 1–6 in a column reach 60
Max, MinSum of the dice. Aim high on Max and low on Min.
(Max−Min)×1Max minus Min, times the number of ones in that column
Straight1-2-3-4-5 or 2-3-4-5-6: 66 on roll 1, 56 on roll 2, 46 on roll 3
3 of a kindThree of a kind: 3 × face + 10
Full houseThree of a kind and a pair: their sum + 30
4 of a kindFour of a kind: 4 × face + 40
YambFive of a kind: 5 × face + 50

Five of a kind also counts as a full house, 4 of a kind or 3 of a kind. Change any of this under House rules.

More columns for house rules

These can be added under “House rules”:

⇅
From the middle. Start at Max or Min, then work outward: up toward the ones, down toward Yamb.
⇵
To the middle. Start at the ones or at Yamb, then work in toward Max and Min.
R
First roll. Only from your first roll.

Ways to play in Yamb Café

  • Play the computer. Up to five opponents, from beginner to Professor.
  • Daily Yamb. Same dice for everyone. One column, about five minutes.
  • Hot seat. One device goes round the table. Up to six players, computer opponents welcome.
  • Multiplayer. Everyone on their own device, live, wherever they are. Up to six.
  • Friend duel. Not live: you play now, your friend answers when it suits them. The same dice for both, so only skill decides.
  • Real dice. Bring real dice to the party. The game is your notebook: it keeps the sheet and adds it all up.
