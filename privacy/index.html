Privacy

Yamb Café · updated 17 November 2026

Yamb Café is a dice game made by Ivan Kostešić, Croatia. This page says what the game keeps about you, where, and why. For questions or requests, write to hello@yambcafe.com.

In short
  • You can play without an account. Then your games stay on your device.
  • If you sign in with Google, the game keeps your name, your finished games and your scores, so they follow you to other devices and your best Daily Yamb scores can appear on the high scores.
  • Visits are counted without cookies and without knowing who you are.
  • No ads, no tracking cookies, and your data is never sold.

Playing without an account

The game saves your games in progress, your settings and your records in your browser's storage on this device. They are not sent to us. Clearing the browser's data for yambcafe.com deletes them.

Signing in with Google

Sign-in is optional. When you sign in, we keep:

  • your Google account's identifier, email address, name and profile picture link, which Google passes on when you sign in;
  • the name you choose for the high scores;
  • your finished games: when, which mode and rules, the score, and whether you won;
  • the records this device had before you first signed in, so they are not lost;
  • for the Daily Yamb, your moves, so that the server can check your score is possible with that day's dice.

Other players see only the name you chose and your Daily Yamb scores on the high scores. Your email address is never shown.

We use this to run the game you signed in for. The legal basis is providing the service you asked for (Article 6(1)(b) of the GDPR).

Games on several devices

Devices in a game connect to each other directly, and the moves pass between them. To find each other they use the free PeerJS server (peerjs.com), which sees the devices' internet addresses while they connect, but not the game.

Visitor numbers

To know how many people play and which parts they use, the website counts visits and a few things done in the game:

  • which page was opened, the site you came from (or that you came through a game link), the game's language, and whether it runs installed;
  • your country (only the country, taken from your internet address), whether the device is a phone, a tablet or a desktop, and the hour of the day;
  • games started and finished in each mode, and for a game: the rule set, the number of players, the computer opponents, how long it took, and how many hints and reactions were used;
  • installing the game, signing in, sharing a link, and looking at or buying Plus.

No cookies are set, and nothing is stored on or read from your device for this. To count each visitor only once a day, the counter in our database mixes your internet address and browser name with a random key that changes every day, and keeps only the resulting one-way code. The code cannot be turned back into your address. The next day the codes and the key are deleted, so visits on different days cannot be linked, and only the day's totals stay. The counts are never linked to your account, even when you are signed in.

For signed-in players we also count, from the finished games their accounts already keep, how many play again the following week. Only the totals are looked at.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in knowing how the game is used, so that we can improve it (Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR).

Services the website uses

  • Cloudflare hosts the website and answers for the yambcafe.com address. Like any web host, it sees your internet address when you open a page.
  • jsDelivr provides a small program for games on several devices, and sees your internet address when it sends that file. The lettering comes from our own site.
  • Supabase stores accounts, scores and the visitor counts, on servers in Frankfurt, Germany. The game also asks it for the day's high scores, signed in or not, so it sees your internet address.
  • Google handles the sign-in itself, under Google's privacy policy.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Deleting it removes your profile, your saved games and your scores at once. Visitor codes are deleted the next day; the daily totals are kept. What is stored on your device stays there until you clear it.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how it is used, by writing to hello@yambcafe.com. You can also delete your account yourself in the game: Settings, Account, Delete my account. If you think your data is handled wrongly, you can complain to the Croatian data protection agency, AZOP, or to the agency in your own country.

Children

Signing in is for people aged 16 and over. Anyone can play without an account.

Changes

If anything here changes, we update this page and the date at the top. The terms and refunds are on their own page.
